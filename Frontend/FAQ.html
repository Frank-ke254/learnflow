Frequently Asked Questions:

How do I enroll in a course?

You can enroll by clicking the "Enroll" button on any course page. Make sure you are signed in first.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, you can request a refund within 14 days of enrollment if you haven’t completed more than 20% of the course.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, upon completing a course, you will receive a certificate that you can download from your dashboard.
